# Dependency map (session 2)

Machine-readable: `DEPENDENCY_MAP.json` (generated by `scripts/graph_check.py` from the isolated build).

Contents per version (`per_version.<ver>.rows.<id>`): tab, age, time, `requires` (AND of OR of AND lists, including the automatic Master Sword clause for adult rows), `after` (direct story/checklist prerequisites), `gives`, `parent` (checklist parent), `linked_prizes`, `linked_quests` (with link mode). `story_ancestors` holds the full indirect prerequisite set of every story/checklist node; `nodes_order` is the order the app indexes them in.

Structural notes
- Story graph: 40 steps; `after` edges only point to earlier steps (checked), so ancestors are computed in one pass at start-up.
- Cross-tab dependencies: Skulltula/Heart/Side/Item rows depend on story steps through `@step` tokens compiled at build time into direct step ids, on items, songs, token counts (`GS:N`), and on other quests.
- Age rules are not edges: adult rows get a Master Sword clause; child rows outside the story tab get a temporary Prelude-of-Light clause while the player is stuck as an adult (see F-009 for the missing edge this relies on).
- Day/night are labels only; the engine has no time edges.
- Known defect in the map: no story step requires MQ-22 (Prelude of Light), although the child-era steps MQ-30 and MQ-34 cannot be done without it (F-009).
